Postdoctoral researcher · Utrecht University

Thales Bertaglia

I study how digital platforms and AI systems shape what people see, buy, and believe—and how we can study these systems from the outside.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Utrecht University, where I work on HUMANads, an ERC project on content monetisation and fairness in platform governance. My background is in computer science, but most of my work now sits somewhere between computational social science, platform governance, and law.
