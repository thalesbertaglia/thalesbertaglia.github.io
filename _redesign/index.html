Postdoctoral researcher · Utrecht University

Thales Bertaglia

I study the creator economy and social media platforms, focusing on how content monetisation, algorithmic systems, and platform regulation interact.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Faculty of Law, Economics, and Governance at Utrecht University. I currently work on the ERC Starting Grant HUMANads, which examines content monetisation and private-law fairness in platform governance.
