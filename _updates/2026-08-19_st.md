We wrapped up the first [ChildSafeAds Shared Task](https://www.codabench.org/competitions/17595/), with a total of 57 participants and 223 submissions working on commercial content and advertising-law compliance in child-facing YouTube videos. The task will be presented at [NLLP](https://nllpw.org/workshop/) @ EMNLP 2026! Check out the task description paper [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19165).
